Participant data (CSV)

All personal data, one row per participant with their ID. You can correct first_name, last_name, email, gender, date_of_birth, height_cm, weight_kg and switch community on/off. Empty cells keep the current value; all other columns are ignored.

Network – who sees whom (CSV)

One row per direction. The first column is the person who sees, the second the person they see. Name columns are only there to help you and are ignored.

The upload replaces the whole network. Everyone who does not appear in the first column sees nobody afterwards. You see all changes before anything is saved.
